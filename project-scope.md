## Problem

Tickets from an online school are done manually by humany agents. The agents have to use canned responses to common queries and are restricted to their 9-5 schedule. This process is slow and it makes the agents do repetitive tasks (that they might not like) and they o not get to focus on the human level tasks that actually need them. This leads to delayed, impersonal and sometimes unhelpful responses for the users.

## Solution

Let AI and automation handled the canned responses and it can better make it personal. Also, improve the agents workflow by getting all the information and letting the AI formalize their responses.

## Ticket Statuses

- **Open** — ticket has been created and is awaiting resolution
- **Resolved** — the issue has been addressed
- **Closed** — ticket is finalized and no further action needed

## Ticket Categories

Each ticket belongs to a single category:

- General questions
- Technical questions
- Refund requests

## User Roles

- **Admin** — the system is deployed with a default admin who can create and manage agents
- **Agent** — created by the admin, handles tickets and works with AI-assisted responses

## Features

- automatic email pulling and sorting
- automatic email responding
- human escalation
- improving responses by the agents
- ai summaries
- ai suggested replies
